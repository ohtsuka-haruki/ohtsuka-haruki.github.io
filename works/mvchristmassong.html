Title : Your Letter Brought Me Back (An Original Christmas Ballad)

[LYRICS]

When I fall asleep

The dark slowly fades

Even those dark rain clouds

Now let the sun shine through

Sitting on the roof

I feel a gentle breeze giving me a caress like a mother

You used to write to me at Christmastime

You used to tell me how you cared about me

You used to be my home long before I knew you were my aunt

Please write your letter back now

When I fall asleep

The dark slowly fades

All the lights from the town

Light up the night sky gold

Leaning on the door

I’ve been waiting for a mail

Through the day, half the night, no letter

You know we used to meet on Christmas Eve,

The day we all enjoyed cupcakes, brownies, pudding, stuffing, roast turkey, and juice

When the sun began to rise

My dreams weren’t just dreams

Outside the door was a note

“Your letter brought me back”
